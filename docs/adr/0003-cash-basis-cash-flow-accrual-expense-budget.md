# Cash Flow is cash basis; Expense and Budget tracking are accrual basis

Cash Flow recognizes money only when it crosses the Cash-pocket boundary: Daily, Saving, and
Emergency are Cash pockets, while Credit Card and Investment are non-cash. Expense and Budget
tracking instead recognize an Expense when it is incurred. Therefore, a Credit Card purchase is an
immediate accrued Expense with no Cash Flow effect, and its later payment from a Cash pocket is money
out but not a second Expense. This separation prevents both delayed spending recognition and double
counting, and it must remain explicit in reports and projections.
