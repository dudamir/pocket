# Budgets use monthly allocations, rollover, and hierarchical earmarks

Budgets are monthly allocations for Expense Categories rather than separate monthly and annual
ledgers. No rollover, Positive-only rollover, and Full rollover support regular spending, sinking
funds, and recurring obligations; optional one-time or recurring Targets recommend monthly
allocations without silently changing them. A Parent Budget includes its descendants and Child
Budgets are earmarks inside, not additions to, the parent, so children inherit the parent's rollover
policy and cannot collectively exceed its allocation. Optional Saving-pocket links connect Budget
availability to Dedicated reserves or General savings without treating planned availability and
physical cash as the same thing.
